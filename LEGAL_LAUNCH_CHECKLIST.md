# Legal launch checklist

All localized legal pages are review drafts. The current build supports a provisional project email through `FLOWELIX_CONTACT_EMAIL`. Complete and approve the full provider record before creating commercial configuration.

- Confirm legal entity, registration and fiscal identifiers, address, responsible data controller and professional contact.
- Approve the actual data inventory, lawful bases, notices, processor arrangements, transfers, retention, security procedures and data-subject request process.
- Have the seller and qualified advisers confirm the B2B and B2C offer terms, checkout wording, precontractual disclosures, accessible delivery, withdrawal and refund handling, complaints and confirmation on a durable medium.
- Approve separately configured prices, tax treatment, invoice obligations, accounting flow and any RO e-Factura process.
- Validate Office Scripts compatibility in licensed Excel. Confirm the terms for Microsoft 365, Copilot Studio, Power Automate and customer-managed n8n as applicable. Confirm brand and asset rights before release.
- Review language consistency, accessibility, retention, support terms and release ownership.

The launch gates are unfulfilled. Current payments are test transactions or simulations, and the app has no live payment path.
